About Us

Founded on a love of the outdoors

FOUNDER / TEAM PHOTO

Adventure Escape SA is an initiative by Liam Daniels who has always had a passion for outdoor adventure and eco-tourism. He noticed that families, tourists, schools and corporate groups struggle to find one place where they can compare and book exciting outdoor activities.

Established in 2024, we operate throughout the Western cape and bring together a variety of professionally guided outdoor experiences for visitors of all ages and experience levels.
