About Us

Founded on a love of the outdoors

FOUNDER / TEAM PHOTO

Adventure Escape SA was started by a small group of Western Cape guides who wanted to make it easier for locals and visitors to get outside. What began as a handful of weekend hikes has grown into a full catalogue of guided packages and individual activities across the region.
